# Breadboard Basics

![Breadboard Basics](/assets/images/slides/4_Breadboard_Wiring_Basics.webp)
{: .mt-6 }

Breadboard is a solderless way to connect electronic components for prototyping. Horizontal holes are linked together, but not across the middle divider ![Horizontal holes are linked together](/assets/images/breadboard-horizontal-holes.webp){: .inline-img}. Vertical bus strips are continuous down the side of the board, but don’t cross the middle divider ![Vertical bus strips are linked together](/assets/images/breadboard-vertical-strips.webp){: .inline-img}.

![Breadboard connections](/assets/images/breadboard-connections.webp)
*You can see exactly which holes are connected to each other if you look behind a breadboard without the adhesive backing. Study the breadboard included in the additional sensors kit, where the adhesive backing has been removed. Be gentle as the metal contacts are loose and might fall out of the breadboard.*
